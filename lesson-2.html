SETA, QCTO and SAQA

Who does what?

As a campus manager, you must know which organisation is responsible for each decision. Do not treat SETA and QCTO requirements as one interchangeable rulebook.

SAQA
Maintains the overall National Qualifications Framework and supports national coordination of qualifications and quality assurance.
QCTO
Oversees the Occupational Qualifications Sub-Framework and quality assures occupational qualifications, including requirements for occupational skills development providers and assessments.
SETA
Works within a particular economic sector to support skills planning, employer participation, workplace learning and funding programmes. Requirements and processes can differ between SETAs.

Manager’s rule: identify the qualification, the responsible authority, the current policy or circular, the evidence required, and the person who must act.
