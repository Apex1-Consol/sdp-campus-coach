The learner journey

Why the full journey matters

A campus manager is responsible for more than getting learners into a classroom. The learner journey must be planned, supported, evidenced and reviewed from first contact through completion or exit.

1. Recruitment and selection

Confirm that the learner meets the programme requirements and that the information provided is accurate and fair.

2. Enrolment and induction

Capture the required records, explain the programme, introduce expectations, and make sure the learner understands support channels, attendance and assessment requirements.

3. Delivery and attendance

Monitor participation, attendance, learning progress, facilitator delivery and early warning signs. Do not wait until the end to discover that a learner has disengaged.

4. Support and intervention

Record concerns, assign an owner, agree an intervention and follow up. Support may involve academic help, accessibility, transport, work placement or referral to another service.

5. Assessment and moderation

Keep evidence that assessment was planned, conducted fairly, moderated where required, and recorded against the applicable outcomes or standards.

6. Completion, certification and exit

Confirm the actual completion and certification process for the programme. Track outstanding evidence, submissions, results, certificates and the learner’s next step.

Manager’s rule: a learner is not “successful” merely because they were enrolled. Track progression, competence, completion, certification and meaningful exit outcomes separately.

Minimum learner record

  • Verified identity and entry requirements
  • Programme and cohort details
  • Attendance and participation
  • Support needs and interventions
  • Assessment, moderation and results evidence
  • Completion, certification and exit outcome
